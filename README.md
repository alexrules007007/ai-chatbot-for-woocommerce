## Installation

### Download the plugin

Download the latest `ai-chatbot-for-woocommerce.zip` from your account:

👉 **[chatbotagent.biz/](https://chatbotagent.biz/)**

### Install in WordPress

1. Log in to your WordPress admin panel
2. Go to **Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin**
3. Click **Choose File** and select the downloaded ZIP
4. Click **Install Now**
5. Click **Activate Plugin**
6. Go to **AI Chatbot → License** and enter your license key
7. Go to **AI Chatbot → Index** and click **Start** to index your products
8. Configure **Settings** and **Design** to your liking

### After Activation

1. **License** — activate your key from [chatbotagent.biz/my-account](https://chatbotagent.biz/my-account/)
2. **Index** — run initial product indexing (runs in background, can close the page)
3. **Settings** — store name, welcome message, store instructions for AI
4. **Design** — customize colors, fonts, position, dark mode, glass effect
5. **GDPR** — configure consent banner text and retention policy
